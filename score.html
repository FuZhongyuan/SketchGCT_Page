Detailed Rating Rubrics

Metric Meaning 5 4 3 2 1
MOS Mean Opinion Score: overall speech naturalness and audio quality. Excellent: natural, clear, fluent, and free of obvious artifacts. Good: mostly natural with minor artifacts or slight prosody issues. Fair: understandable, but unnaturalness or artifacts are clearly noticeable. Poor: frequent artifacts, unstable prosody, or tiring listening experience. Bad: severely degraded, very unnatural, or difficult to understand.
SS Speaker Similarity: how similar the generated speaker identity is to the prompt audio. Very similar: sounds like the same speaker with consistent timbre and style. Similar: mostly same speaker, with small differences in timbre or speaking style. Moderately similar: some speaker traits match, but mismatch is obvious. Weakly similar: only a few traits match and it likely sounds like another speaker. Not similar: speaker identity is not preserved.
FSC F0 Sketch Correlation: how well the generated pitch contour follows the given F0 sketch. Excellent match: pitch rises, falls, emphasis, and local shape closely follow the sketch. Good match: main pitch trend follows the sketch with minor local deviations. Partial match: broad trend is recognizable, but several segments deviate. Weak match: only occasional pitch movements align with the sketch. No match: pitch contour is unrelated to the sketch.
ESC Energy Sketch Correlation: how well the generated energy contour follows the given energy sketch. Excellent match: loudness, stress, and energy changes closely follow the sketch. Good match: main energy trend follows the sketch with minor local deviations. Partial match: broad energy pattern is recognizable, but several segments deviate. Weak match: only occasional stress or energy changes align with the sketch. No match: energy contour is unrelated to the sketch.
